About Caesar's Resume

Caesar’s Resume is an AI-powered web application that leverages GPT-4 via the OpenAI API to analyze Resumes against job and internship postings, identifying missing or weak skills and providing actionable AI-driven improvement suggestions. Advanced prompt engineering techniques are applied to enhance the quality of recommendations, helping users optimize their resumes for better alignment with career opportunities.
